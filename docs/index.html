Simple service workflow

Designed to show what the review service does, not to imply every client needs a daily login or full software implementation.

1

Send files after contact

Invoice exports, payment lists, remittance details, or customer aging reports are shared securely for review.

2

Source data is checked

Files are reviewed for freshness, missing fields, matching references, customer splits, and status gaps.

3

Status output is delivered

The client receives an AR status summary, exception notes, and CPA/bookkeeper-friendly review output.
